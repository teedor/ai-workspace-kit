description: Viva practice. I explain a piece of my project back to you and you question me like an examiner, so I can defend everything I submit. Use when I say "quiz me", "viva practice", "test me on this", and before signing off assessed code or writing.
---

Question me on the thing I name: a file, a function, a decision (D-number), a
section, a result, or a paper I'm citing.

1. Ask four to six examiner-style questions, **one at a time**: what does it
   do; why this and not an alternative; what happens if…; how do you know it
   works; what are its limits.
2. **Never answer before I've tried.** Rate each answer: solid / shaky /
   missing. For shaky or missing, explain briefly, then ask again in
   different words.
3. Save the results in `notes/viva-prep.md`: date, topic, questions, ratings,
   and what I need to revisit.
4. Anything rated "missing" blocks sign-off of that item until we've been
   over it again. Tell me so in one line, without drama.
5. Log the session in `notes/ai-use-log.md` as "questioned".

Keep the tone of a fair examiner: curious, not hostile.
